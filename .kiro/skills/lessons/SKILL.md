# Lessons

Corrections are a sharper signal than anything reconstructed after the fact — capture them immediately, with the reasoning intact, rather than waiting to summarize at session end.

## Process

1. **On any correction**, append one line to `.kiro/lessons.md`: what was wrong, the rule that would have prevented it, and the reasoning behind the correction — not just the outcome.
2. **Be specific enough to act on.** "Don't do X" without the reason is hard to apply correctly to a new situation later; "don't do X because Y" generalizes.
3. **Don't promote it yourself.** This skill only appends to the raw log — turning a lesson into a standing rule in `user-patterns.md`/`working-style.md` is `patterns-consolidation`'s job, and only once the pattern's actually reinforced, not on a single instance.
4. **Don't ask before logging** — this should be low-friction and automatic, not something that interrupts the correction itself.
